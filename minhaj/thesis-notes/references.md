# Key References

The thesis background should discuss the following topics and papers:

- FedAvg for communication-efficient federated learning.
- FedProx for optimization under non-IID client data.
- LoRA for parameter-efficient fine-tuning.
- FFA-LoRA for freezing the A projection during aggregation.
- CORN for ordinal regression with rank-consistent thresholds.
- FedPer and FedRep for personalized federated representations.
- Deep-SE and GPT2SP for story point estimation.
- TAWOS and its replication studies for the software engineering dataset and empirical context.

These references support the method design, the non-IID motivation, and the comparison with existing story point estimation approaches.
